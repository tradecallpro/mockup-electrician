Accessibility Statement

Moore Electric is committed to making this website usable for all visitors, including people with disabilities. We work toward conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA.

Measures we take

  • Pages use a clear heading structure and semantic page regions, with a "Skip to main content" link as the first item on the page.
  • Informative images have text descriptions (alt text). Decorative images are marked so screen readers skip them.
  • Text and background colors are chosen to meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios.
  • Menus, buttons, forms, the photo gallery, and pop-up windows can all be used with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus indicator. Pressing Escape closes pop-ups and returns you to where you were.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and messages are written out in text, not shown by color alone.
  • Buttons and links on phones and tablets are at least 44 pixels in size, so they are easy to tap.
  • Animations and smooth scrolling are turned off for visitors whose device is set to reduce motion. Nothing plays automatically.
  • Icon-only controls, such as the menu and close buttons, have text labels for screen readers.

Known limitations

Some content may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA. This site includes content from third parties, such as the fonts, the voice demo, and links to social media. We do not control the accessibility of third-party content. We correct limitations on our own content as we find them.
