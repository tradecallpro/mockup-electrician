When Does a Bakersfield Home Need a Panel Upgrade?

Sample post for illustration. Not written by Moore Electric.

Most Bakersfield homeowners never think about the electrical panel until something goes wrong. The breakers start tripping, the lights flicker when the A/C kicks on, or the electrician says the words "your panel is full." Here is how to tell if your home is due for an upgrade.

1. Breakers that keep tripping

A breaker that trips once in a while is doing its job. One that trips every week, or a panel that feels warm or smells off, is telling you something. An electrician can check whether it is an overloaded circuit, a worn breaker, or a panel that has reached the end of its life.

2. An older panel

Many homes in Bakersfield and across Kern County still run panels from decades ago, built for a lot less power than a modern home uses. Newer panels handle today's loads and give you room to add circuits later.

3. Adding an EV charger or a spa

A home EV charger or a spa needs its own dedicated circuit, and sometimes the panel simply has no room or not enough capacity. If you are planning either one, a panel check should come first. The same goes for new A/C or mini split power.

4. What an upgrade involves

A good upgrade means a proper permit, the new panel set and wired, every breaker labeled, and the power back on the same day in most cases. Ask for a free estimate before you decide. Serving Bakersfield, Tehachapi, Shafter, Delano, and the rest of Kern County.
